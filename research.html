Research

I work on the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider at CERN, searching for new fundamental particles beyond the Standard Model. My work combines data analysis and machine learning with hands-on detector development — building and commissioning the hardware that will power the next generation of high-energy collisions.

Current & recent projects
01
Vector-Like Leptons
Searching for vector-like leptons — exotic heavy particles predicted by several beyond-Standard-Model theories. Using proton–proton collision data from ATLAS and machine learning classifiers to distinguish rare signal events from overwhelming Standard Model backgrounds.

02
ITk Interlock System
Working on the safety interlock system for the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk), the next-generation silicon detector being built for the High-Luminosity LHC. The interlock system protects the detector from damage during operation — critical infrastructure for a piece of equipment that cannot be easily accessed once installed.
