Upgrading the ATLAS Silicon Tracker

The High-Luminosity LHC will deliver far more collisions than the LHC does today, and ATLAS is replacing its inner tracker with a new all-silicon detector, the ITk, to cope. I work on the safety interlock systems that protect it and on the common electronics shared across the detector.

Searching for new physics at the LHC

I search for new fundamental particles beyond the Standard Model in ATLAS proton–proton collision data, using machine learning to pick out rare signals from overwhelming backgrounds. My main focus is a search for vector-like leptons, heavy particles that appear in several extensions of the Standard Model.
