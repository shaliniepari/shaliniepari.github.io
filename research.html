Research

I work on the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider, searching for new fundamental particles beyond the Standard Model. My work combines data analysis and machine learning with hands-on detector development for the next generation of high-energy collisions.

Detector hardware

ITk interlock system

Safety interlock system for the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk), the next-generation silicon detector being built for the High-Luminosity LHC. The interlock protects the detector during operation: critical infrastructure for an instrument that cannot easily be reached once it is installed.

New physics

Vector-like leptons

A search for exotic heavy particles predicted by several theories beyond the Standard Model, using ATLAS proton–proton collision data and machine-learning classifiers to separate rare signal events from much larger Standard Model backgrounds.
