01 About me

I'm a postdoctoral researcher on the ATLAS experiment at CERN, searching for new particles predicted by theories beyond the Standard Model — specifically vector-like leptons. The Standard Model is a spectacular theory, but it leaves too many questions unanswered.

Most of my work is a mix of experimental physics, statistics, and machine learning: building classifiers to pick signals out of enormous backgrounds, designing analysis strategies, and asking uncomfortable questions about whether the answer is real.

02 My research
BSM Physics
Vector-Like Leptons
Searching for vector-like leptons — exotic particles predicted by several beyond-Standard-Model theories — in proton–proton collision data from the ATLAS detector. These would be among the first signs of new physics at the LHC.

Detector R&D
ITk Interlock System
Working on the safety interlock system for the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk), CERN's next-generation silicon strip and pixel detector being built for the High-Luminosity LHC upgrade.

03 Playgrounds

Physics gets a reputation for being impenetrable. I don't think it has to be. These are tools I've built to make some of the ideas tangible — the kind of thing I wish I'd had earlier. More are always in the works.

Interactive · Machine learning
Neural network playground
A neural network classifier built entirely from scratch in JavaScript — no libraries, just the raw maths. Paint signal and background points, dial up the layers and neurons, hit train, and watch the decision boundary carve through your data in real time.
